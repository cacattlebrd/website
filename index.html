For bakeries and food plants

Stop paying to throw product away. We pay for usable by-products. We can pick them up at your dock, or you can drop them off at our plant.

  1. Call or email us

    Tell us what you make, roughly how much by-product you have and where you're located.

  2. We set the price

    Our price is tied to the corn market, so you get a fair rate that moves with feed values.

  3. We get it moving

    We spot a trailer at your dock, or you drop loads at our plant. Whichever works for you.

  4. You get paid monthly

    Every load is weighed and logged. You get a statement and a check each month.

Good for the planet, good for your report card

Sending by-products to cattle instead of a landfill is one of the best things a food plant can do for its environmental numbers, and it's simple to show customers, auditors and corporate.

24%

of everything in U.S. landfills is food waste.

58%

of landfill methane comes from that food waste. Methane traps far more heat than CO₂.

Source: U.S. EPA, Quantifying Methane Emissions from Landfilled Food Waste (2023) and Advancing Sustainable Materials Management.
